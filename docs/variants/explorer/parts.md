# Parts & tools

## Parts

The [bill of materials](https://github.com/TH4R4V3N1324/hexapod-hardware/blob/main/explorer/bom.csv) in hexapod-hardware is the
parts list for the Explorer v0.1, with quantities and suppliers. The main
components are:

- 18 × ST3215 serial bus servos and a Waveshare Bus Servo Adapter (A)
- Raspberry Pi 5 with active cooler and microSD card
- 2 × [leg hub](https://github.com/TH4R4V3N1324/hexapod-hardware/tree/main/electronics/leg-hub) PCBs
- RPLIDAR A2M12 lidar, Arducam ToF camera, Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 and
  ISM330DHCX IMU
- 3S Li-ion battery pack, BMS and charger
- A latching power switch and a two-contact normally-closed E-stop
- Six 55 mm 1/10-scale shocks, 4 mm linear rods and LM4UU bearings for the
  sprung tibias
- Connectors, wire, fuses and fasteners
- Filament: a rigid filament for the structure, PETG for the tibia
  cartridges and TPU for the feet (see [Printing](printing.md))

## Tools

- A 3D printer that can print PETG and TPU (and PA12-CF, if you use it for the
  legs)
- A soldering iron with a heat-set insert tip, for the M3 inserts
- A crimp tool for Molex Mini-SPOX and JST PH terminals, such as an Engineer
  PA-09 or IWISS SN-2549
- Hex keys for M3 and M2.5 screws
- A way to cut 4 mm hardened steel rod to length
- A heat gun for heatshrink
